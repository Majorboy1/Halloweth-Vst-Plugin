# Guitar, piano, synth and Afrobeats sound sources

Reviewed 8 November 2026. Product prices and licenses can change; check the
linked vendor pages before purchase or redistribution.

## Guitar instruments

| Use | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Realistic strummed rhythm parts | [Native Instruments Session Guitarist – Strummed Acoustic](https://www.native-instruments.com/products/session-guitarist-strummed-acoustic/) | Recorded steel-string performances, chord-aware patterns, and real-time pattern control. Runs in Kontakt Player. |
| More control over notes and guitar articulations | [MusicLab RealGuitar 6](https://www.musiclab.com/products/realguitar/info) | Steel and nylon models, sampled strings/frets, solo and accompaniment performance modes, and a 15-day trial. |
| Guitar-like sounds inside Halloweth | Factory **Velvet Nylon Guitar** and **Steel String Glow** | Bundled Meta Open Sound CC0 samples; the steel/nylon guitar banks are available directly in the factory library. |

NI's current Strummed Acoustic page lists $99 and an 8.5 GB download; RealGuitar
lists a roughly 1.3 GB installer. Those are separate third-party instruments,
not sounds that can be copied into Halloweth.

## Omnisphere-style sound design

[Omnisphere](https://www.spectrasonics.net/products/omnisphere/overview.php) is
Spectrasonics' own licensed synth and library. Its patch names, patches, and
sample content are not included here and must not be extracted or repackaged.
For a similar broad workflow, [Arturia Pigments](https://www.arturia.com/products/software-instruments/pigments/overview)
offers wavetable, granular, sample, harmonic, modal, and virtual-analog engines
with a large preset library. Halloweth can also make original layered synth
patches with its own oscillators, filter, LFO, and effects.

## Afrobeats drum sources

HALLOWETH now ships five 16-pad kits, including **Afrobeats Essentials**,
**Amapiano Bounce**, **Afro Gospel Praise**, **Afro Percussion Ensemble**, and
**Highlife Guitar Band**. Their kicks, snares, hats, hand drums, and
percussion are curated from the CC0 [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL).
Sample provenance, the pinned source revision, and the license are bundled in
[`Resources/DrumKits`](../Resources/DrumKits/README.md). Load **Afrobeats Kit**,
**Amapiano Kit**, **Highlife Kit**, **Gospel Percussion**, or **Afro Percussion**
from the preset browser to start playing.

- [LABADI's Afrobeats one-shots](https://soundza.com/packs/afrobeats-drum-and-percussion-one-shots)
  is a commercial 24-bit WAV source for percussion-heavy kits. Its Soundza Standard
  License permits use in finished tracks but forbids redistribution as a kit,
  so the source is documented for users rather than copied into Halloweth.
- [Splice's Afrobeats drum catalog](https://splice.com/sounds/genres/afrobeat/drums/samples)
  offers more licensed choices; its samples are for making tracks, not for
  repackaging as a plugin library.

Load a legally obtained WAV pack onto the DRUMS page's 16 pads, then save the
kit in Halloweth's JSON format. This also lets you customize the bundled kits.
