# Factory drum kits

HALLOWETH includes five ready-to-play, 16-pad kits for Afrobeat-oriented production:

- **Afrobeats Essentials** pairs a tight drum foundation with bongo, conga, shaker, agogo, cowbell, and guiro hits.
- **Amapiano Bounce** uses lower tuned kick and hand-drum pads with space for the producer's own log-drum instrument.
- **Highlife Guitar Band** combines live-style drums, bell patterns, conga, cabasa, and tambourine.
- **Afro Gospel Praise** adds a full band foundation with hand drums, shakers, tambourine, and bell accents.
- **Afro Percussion Ensemble** focuses on tuned hand drums, bongo, conga, shaker, and bell patterns.

Load one from the preset browser (**Afrobeats Kit**, **Amapiano Kit**, **Gospel Percussion**, **Highlife Kit**, or **Afro Percussion**) and open the DRUMS page to audition and edit the pads. The kits are also available as editable JSON files in their genre folders. Each kit has 16 pads on MIDI notes 36-51.

## Sample source and rights

Bundled one-shots are unmodified WAV files from the [Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL), pinned to commit [`c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e`](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/tree/c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e). VCSL is released under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/blob/c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e/LICENSE); its license is included as `VCSL-LICENSE.txt`. `SOURCES.md` maps each packaged WAV to its original VCSL path, and `SHA256SUMS` records the packaged file hashes.

These are CC0 general-purpose and Latin percussion recordings curated into Afrobeat-oriented kits; the kits do not claim that the source recordings are African field recordings. The kits are mappings and mix settings made for HALLOWETH.

## External sample packs

- [LABADI: Afrobeats Drum and Percussion One-Shots](https://soundza.com/packs/afrobeats-drum-and-percussion-one-shots) offers a larger commercial sound palette. Its [Standard License](https://soundza.com/license) permits using samples in finished music but prohibits redistributing the sounds as a sample kit, so it is not bundled.
- [Splice Afrobeats drum samples](https://splice.com/sounds/genres/afrobeat/drums/samples) provides a paid catalog; check current license terms before redistribution.
